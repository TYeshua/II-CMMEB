# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Vite, with Three.js for the 3D loader and hero (requested by the user). The original single-file site stays in `legacy/index.html` as reference.

## Users

- Researchers, graduate and undergraduate students in ocean sciences, engineering, geosciences, biology, law, economics and social sciences who would attend or submit work.
- Professionals of the productive sector (offshore energy, environmental consulting, ports, offshore services) and regulators.
- Companies considering sponsorship.

## Product Purpose

Landing page of the II CMMEB — II Congresso de Ciências do Mar na Margem Equatorial Brasileira, Salinópolis – PA, 2027. Central theme: "Integração Indústria e Meio Ambiente". Success = a visitor understands what, when and where, and goes to register (inscrição). Secondary actions: submit work, sponsor.

## Positioning

The only congress dedicated to the Brazilian Equatorial Margin (Amapá to Rio Grande do Norte): the new energy frontier and one of the least studied Atlantic coasts (Amazon plume reefs, the largest continuous mangrove belt in the world, coastal communities). It puts people who study this ocean and people who operate in it in the same room.

## Operating Context

- First edition: São Luís, October 2024 (31 talks, 3 round tables, 3 poster sessions, 22 institutions, 30 speakers). Preceded by 2023 workshops (71 researchers + 10 Petrobras representatives).
- Eight thematic axes guide submissions.
- Organised by Departamento de Oceanografia e Limnologia, UFMA; 2024 realisation with Fundação Sousândrade, AOCEANO Norte and Petrobras.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dates (22–24 Sept 2027), 2027 programme, prices, exact venue, 2027 speakers and the registration timeline are placeholders to be confirmed. Prices are "em breve".
- No registration backend yet: actions point to sections / future links.
- Official domain: cmmeb.com.br.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the existing colour palette (paper #f2f9fb, foam #cdeef5, sky #8fd3e8, surf #2a9fbd, sea #1b6ba3, ink #0b2440, deep blues #0c4d79 → #06142a, sun #f5a524, dusk #e0622a, guará red #d33a2c) as the base.
- Logo mark: sun, sea arc with wave bands, guará (scarlet ibis) arc, platform.
- No light/dark toggle: the page is a descent from surface (light) to deep sea (dark).
- Prefer unique illustrations and 3D over photography; credited open-licence photographs are allowed as support.

## Evidence on Hand

- 2024 line-up (30 speakers, institutions, talk titles), 2024 numbers, axes with 2024 talks, FAQ answers — all in `legacy/index.html`.
- No testimonials, attendance figures for 2027, sponsors for 2027 or press. Do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Industry and environment are shown together, never as opposing camps.
2. Facts about the Equatorial Margin are specific and true (basins, currents, tides, ecosystems).
3. Provisional information is always marked as provisional.
4. Registration is always one step away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Portuguese (pt-BR). Respect prefers-reduced-motion; 3D must have a static fallback; full keyboard access.
